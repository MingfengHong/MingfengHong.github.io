## Portrait prompt

Make ONE very simple black-and-white hand-drawn cartoon mascot for a personal website. PRIMARY reference is the tiny cartoon man with glasses and laptop INSIDE the portfolio preview in the FIRST attached screenshot. Match that level of abstraction and simplicity, NOT the screenshot layout. SECOND attachment is a photo only to identify FOUR broad traits: short side-swept black hair, round-ish head, glasses, young male. DO NOT recreate, trace, render, stylize, or preserve the real person's actual facial anatomy or likeness. Invent a new generic, charming, highly simplified Notion-style cartoon using those four traits. Two dot eyes behind large simple rounded rectangular glasses. A nose drawn with just one small bent line. A smile drawn with one short curve. Absolutely no iris detail, eyelids, eye bags, cheek lines, lips, nostrils, skin texture, realistic ears, realistic facial proportions or face-specific details. Small simplified head atop a larger easy silhouette body, natural cartoon proportions, front upright head, not the photograph's tilt. Half-body in a loose white short-sleeve collared shirt, holding a small open laptop, facing slightly left. Hands minimal mitten-like fingers. Thin black slightly wobbly single strokes, black hair as one simple solid shape, white fills. Last three references only guide organic hand-drawn lines. It should read as a friendly hand-drawn SYMBOL OF A RESEARCHER, not a portrait of a real person. Transparent outer background, opaque white inside the illustration. No color, gradient, shadows, realistic rendering, border, scene, text, typography or watermark. Entire character including elbows visible with generous margins. 4:5 composition.

## Notebook prompt

Create one minimal monochrome editorial line drawing for a researcher's Notion-style website: an open research notebook with a few abstract short ink lines (not readable text), a pencil lying diagonally beside it, and one small four-point hand-drawn spark above. References are ONLY for organic slightly uneven rounded hand-drawn ink lines, not composition or their depicted objects. Broad simple expressive forms, understated and approachable. Fine-to-medium black ink lines, white opaque paper fill, transparent background around the objects. No surrounding shape or carrier, no color, no photorealism, no gradients, no shading, no shadows, no text, no logos. A relaxed 3/4 view, horizontal compact composition, 15% clear margins. It should work as a restrained 120px illustration beside a page title. The user's monochrome Notion portfolio reference overrides the skill's default colored background.
